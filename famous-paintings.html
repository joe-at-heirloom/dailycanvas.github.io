Famous paintings: 47 masterpieces everyone should know

The most famous paintings in the world include the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper by Leonardo da Vinci, The Starry Night by Vincent van Gogh, Girl with a Pearl Earring by Johannes Vermeer, The Birth of Venus by Sandro Botticelli, The Creation of Adam by Michelangelo, The Scream by Edvard Munch, The Kiss by Gustav Klimt and The Great Wave off Kanagawa by Hokusai. Below are 47 of the world's most famous paintings in date order: who painted each one, when, where you can see it today and why it matters. Every one is in the public domain, so each also comes as a free iPhone and Mac wallpaper.

The Renaissance, 1430s to 1560s

Oil paint, perspective and the rediscovery of ancient Greece and Rome turned painting into the art of the age, from Florence and Rome to Bruges and Antwerp.

1. The Arnolfini Portrait

Jan van Eyck · 1434 · Oil on oak panel

National Gallery, London

A merchant and his wife stand in a room in Bruges, above a convex mirror that reflects two more people in the doorway. Van Eyck signed it on the wall in Latin: Jan van Eyck was here, 1434.

Questions

What is the most famous painting in the world?

Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa, in the Louvre in Paris, is generally considered the most famous painting in the world. Close behind are Van Gogh's The Starry Night, Michelangelo's The Creation of Adam, Leonardo's The Last Supper, Vermeer's Girl with a Pearl Earring, Botticelli's The Birth of Venus and Munch's The Scream.

Why is the Mona Lisa so famous?

It combines Leonardo's reputation, a subtle smile and gaze painted with soft, smoky blending, and a dramatic history. Its fame grew enormously after an Italian handyman stole it from the Louvre in 1911; it was missing for over two years and its empty spot on the wall drew crowds.

What is the most expensive painting ever sold?

Salvator Mundi, a painting of Christ attributed to Leonardo da Vinci, sold at Christie's in New York in November 2017 for $450.3 million, the highest price ever paid for a painting at auction.

Where can I see the most famous paintings?

Many are in a handful of museums: the Louvre in Paris (Mona Lisa, Liberty Leading the People, The Raft of the Medusa), the Uffizi in Florence (The Birth of Venus, Primavera), the Prado in Madrid (Las Meninas), the National Gallery in London (Sunflowers, The Arnolfini Portrait), and in New York the Museum of Modern Art (The Starry Night) and The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
