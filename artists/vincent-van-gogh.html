Vincent van Gogh: paintings, life and where to see them

Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890) was a Dutch painter whose bold color and thick, swirling brushwork made him the best-known Post-Impressionist. He painted for only about ten years and made most of his famous work in the last two, in the south of France.

Life

The son of a Dutch pastor, Van Gogh worked for the art dealers Goupil & Cie in The Hague, London and Paris, then tried teaching and preaching before deciding, at 27, to become an artist. His early pictures of peasants and weavers are dark and earthy; The Potato Eaters (1885) is the best known.

In 1886 he moved to Paris to live with his brother Theo, an art dealer, and discovered the Impressionists and Japanese prints; his palette brightened at once. In February 1888 he left for Arles in Provence, where he painted sunflowers, orchards, the Yellow House and the café terraces, and where Paul Gauguin joined him that autumn. The visit ended in December with a breakdown in which Van Gogh cut off his left ear.

He spent a year in the asylum at Saint-Rémy, where he painted The Starry Night, and moved to Auvers-sur-Oise near Paris in May 1890. He died there on July 29, 1890, at 37, two days after a gunshot wound generally believed to be self-inflicted. Theo died six months later, and Theo's widow, Jo van Gogh-Bonger, spent decades building his reputation.

Where to see Van Gogh's work

The Van Gogh Museum in Amsterdam holds the largest collection of his work, and the Kröller-Müller Museum in Otterlo the second largest. In the US, MoMA has The Starry Night, and the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago and the National Gallery of Art each have several of his paintings.

The paintings on this page: Art Institute of Chicago (6); National Gallery of Art, Washington, DC (5); The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York (3); Cleveland Museum of Art (2); Courtauld Gallery, London (1); Kröller-Müller Museum, Otterlo (1); Museum of Modern Art, New York (1); National Gallery, London (1).

Questions

What is Van Gogh's most famous painting?

The Starry Night (1889), painted at the asylum in Saint-Rémy and now at the Museum of Modern Art in New York. His Sunflowers and Café Terrace at Night are close behind.

How many paintings did Van Gogh make?

About 860 oil paintings, among more than 2,000 works including drawings and watercolors, most of them made in the last two years of his life.

Where can I see Van Gogh's paintings?

The Van Gogh Museum in Amsterdam and the Kröller-Müller Museum in Otterlo have the largest collections. In the US: MoMA, the Met, the Art Institute of Chicago and the National Gallery of Art.
