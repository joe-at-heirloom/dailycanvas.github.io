Thomas Cole: paintings, life and where to see them

Thomas Cole (1801–1848) was an English-born American painter who founded the Hudson River School, the first American movement in painting. His landscapes of the Catskills and New England, and his allegorical series, made the wilderness a national subject.

Life

Cole emigrated from England with his family in 1818 and taught himself to paint while working as an engraver and portrait painter in Ohio and Pennsylvania. In 1825 he sailed up the Hudson River to sketch, and the landscapes he brought back to New York sold at once to the painters John Trumbull and Asher B. Durand.

He painted the Catskills, the White Mountains and Niagara, and traveled in England and Italy. His five-part The Course of Empire (1833–36) traces a civilization from wilderness to ruin, and The Oxbow (1836) sets cultivated land against storm and forest.

His four-part The Voyage of Life (1840, painted again in 1842) follows a life from childhood to old age. He lived at Cedar Grove in Catskill, New York, now the Thomas Cole National Historic Site, and died there in 1848, at 47.

Where to see Cole's work

The Met has The Oxbow, the New-York Historical Society The Course of Empire, and the National Gallery of Art the second set of The Voyage of Life. His house and studio in Catskill are open to visitors.

The paintings on this page: National Gallery of Art, Washington, DC (9); Art Institute of Chicago (2); Cleveland Museum of Art (2); Smithsonian American Art Museum, Washington, DC (2).

Questions

What is Thomas Cole's most famous painting?

The Oxbow (1836), at the Met: a view from Mount Holyoke in Massachusetts over a bend in the Connecticut River.

What is the Hudson River School?

America's first homegrown school of painting, from about 1825 to 1875: large, luminous landscapes of the Hudson Valley, the Catskills and later the West. Thomas Cole founded it; Frederic Church and Albert Bierstadt followed.

What is The Voyage of Life?

A series of four paintings, Childhood, Youth, Manhood and Old Age, showing a traveler's boat on the river of life. Cole painted it in 1840 and again in 1842; the second set is at the National Gallery of Art.
