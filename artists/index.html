Famous artists: 25 painters everyone should know

The most famous artists in history include Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Rembrandt, Vincent van Gogh, Claude Monet, Pablo Picasso and Frida Kahlo. This guide covers 25 great painters whose work is in the public domain, from Botticelli and Caravaggio to Monet, Van Gogh and Matisse, each with their life, their best-known paintings and where to see them.

Questions

Who is the most famous artist of all time?

There's no single answer, but Leonardo da Vinci is the name most often given, for the Mona Lisa and The Last Supper. Michelangelo, Rembrandt, Vincent van Gogh and Pablo Picasso are the others most often named.

Who are the most famous Impressionist painters?

Claude Monet, Pierre-Auguste Renoir, Edgar Degas, Camille Pissarro, Berthe Morisot, Alfred Sisley and Mary Cassatt, with Édouard Manet as their forerunner.

Who are the most famous American painters?

Among 19th-century painters: Winslow Homer, John Singer Sargent, Mary Cassatt, James McNeill Whistler, Thomas Cole and Albert Bierstadt. In the 20th century: Edward Hopper, Georgia O'Keeffe, Jackson Pollock and Andy Warhol.

Why aren't Picasso, Frida Kahlo or Dalí in this guide?

Their work is still under copyright, and every picture on this site is in the public domain, so it can be shown in full and downloaded free. Leonardo, Michelangelo, Raphael and Rembrandt appear in our famous paintings guide.
