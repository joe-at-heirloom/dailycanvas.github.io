Renaissance art: the paintings, the artists and how to recognize it

Renaissance art is the art of Italy from the early 1400s to about 1600, when painters revived the ideals of ancient Greece and Rome and used perspective, anatomy and oil paint to make pictures look real. Its greatest names are Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Raphael, Botticelli and Titian.

c. 1400–1600

The Northern Renaissance

History

The Renaissance, from the French for "rebirth", began in Florence in the early 1400s, when artists and scholars set out to revive the art and learning of ancient Greece and Rome. Filippo Brunelleschi worked out linear perspective, and Masaccio used it in his frescoes of the 1420s to make painted space convincingly real.

Through the 15th century, painters such as Fra Angelico, Filippo Lippi, Piero della Francesca and Sandro Botticelli, supported by patrons like the Medici, combined perspective with close study of the human body and of classical myth. Oil painting, perfected in Flanders, reached Venice, where Giovanni Bellini used it for luminous color.

Around 1500 the High Renaissance reached its peak in Florence and Rome with Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo and Raphael, while in Venice Giorgione and Titian made color and light the heart of painting. After the Sack of Rome in 1527, younger artists turned to the stylized elegance of Mannerism.

How to recognize it

Balanced, symmetrical compositions; one-point perspective; idealized figures; classical architecture and myth alongside Christian subjects.

Key artists: Masaccio, Sandro Botticelli, Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Raphael, Titian.

Questions

What are the most famous Renaissance paintings?

Leonardo da Vinci's Mona Lisa and The Last Supper, Michelangelo's The Creation of Adam on the Sistine Chapel ceiling, Botticelli's The Birth of Venus and Primavera, and Raphael's The School of Athens.

Who are the most important Renaissance painters?

Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo and Raphael, the three masters of the High Renaissance, with Sandro Botticelli, Titian, Giovanni Bellini, Masaccio and Piero della Francesca.

When was the Renaissance?

In Italian painting, from the early 1400s to about 1600, at its height from about 1490 to 1527. It spread north to Flanders, Germany and France during the 1400s and 1500s.
