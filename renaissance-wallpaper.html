Questions

Are these wallpapers free to download?

Yes. Every painting here is in the public domain or released under CC0 by the museum that holds it, so you can download and use it as your wallpaper for free.

How do I set a painting as my iPhone wallpaper?

Tap iPhone under the painting and save the image to Photos. Then open Settings, tap Wallpaper, tap Add New Wallpaper, choose Photos and pick the painting. The download is already 1290 × 2796, the size of a large iPhone screen.

How do I set a painting as my Mac desktop?

Tap Mac under the painting to download it, then right-click the file in Finder and choose Set Desktop Picture. The Mac download is 3456 × 2234, cropped for a MacBook Pro display.

Can my iPhone wallpaper change to a new painting every day?

Yes. Daily Canvas, a free app, picks a new painting each morning and sets it as your wallpaper. On iPhone it uses a Shortcuts automation that takes about 30 seconds to set up once; on Mac it changes your desktop on its own.
