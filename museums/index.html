The best art museums in the US

The best art museums in the US include The Metropolitan Museum of Art and MoMA in New York, the Art Institute of Chicago, the National Gallery of Art in Washington, the Museum of Fine Arts in Boston and the Getty in Los Angeles. Here are 14 worth planning a trip around, with what to see at each one and which ones are free.

1. The Metropolitan Museum of Art

New York · founded 1870

The largest art museum in the US, with five thousand years of art from every part of the world on Fifth Avenue. Its European paintings galleries alone could fill a day.

What to see:

  • Washington Crossing the Delaware (Emanuel Leutze)
  • Wheat Field with Cypresses (Vincent van Gogh)
  • Madame X (John Singer Sargent)
  • The Harvesters (Pieter Bruegel the Elder)
  • the Temple of Dendur

2. Art Institute of Chicago

Chicago · founded 1879

One of the great Impressionist and Post-Impressionist collections outside Paris, alongside the most famous American paintings of the 20th century.

What to see:

  • A Sunday on La Grande Jatte (Georges Seurat)
  • Nighthawks (Edward Hopper)
  • American Gothic (Grant Wood)
  • The Bedroom (Vincent van Gogh)
  • Paris Street; Rainy Day (Gustave Caillebotte)

Famous paintings at The Art InstituteThe museum's website

3. National Gallery of Art

Washington, DC · founded 1937 · Free

Founded with Andrew Mellon's gift to the nation and free to everyone, on the National Mall. It has the only painting by Leonardo da Vinci in the Americas.

What to see:

  • Ginevra de' Benci (Leonardo da Vinci)
  • Woman with a Parasol (Claude Monet)
  • Girl with the Red Hat (Johannes Vermeer)
  • Watson and the Shark (John Singleton Copley)
  • Little Girl in a Blue Armchair (Mary Cassatt)

Famous paintings at The National GalleryThe museum's website

4. Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)

New York · founded 1929

The museum that defined modern art for the world, from Post-Impressionism to the present.

What to see:

  • The Starry Night (Vincent van Gogh)
  • Les Demoiselles d'Avignon (Pablo Picasso)
  • The Persistence of Memory (Salvador Dalí)
  • Water Lilies (Claude Monet)
  • Hope II (Gustav Klimt)

The museum's website

5. Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

Boston · founded 1870

One of the largest collections in the country, with superb Impressionists, American paintings and one of the finest collections of Japanese art outside Japan.

What to see:

  • Where Do We Come From? What Are We? Where Are We Going? (Paul Gauguin)
  • The Slave Ship (J. M. W. Turner)
  • The Daughters of Edward Darley Boit (John Singer Sargent)
  • Paul Revere (John Singleton Copley)

The museum's website

6. Cleveland Museum of Art

Cleveland · founded 1913 · Free

Free to everyone since it opened in 1916, with a collection of remarkable depth for its city's size.

What to see:

  • The Burning of the Houses of Lords and Commons (J. M. W. Turner)
  • Twilight in the Wilderness (Frederic Edwin Church)
  • The Crucifixion of Saint Andrew (Caravaggio)
  • La Vie (Pablo Picasso)

Famous paintings at The Cleveland Museum of ArtThe museum's website

7. Detroit Institute of Arts

Detroit · founded 1885 · Free for residents of Wayne, Oakland and Macomb counties

Diego Rivera's Detroit Industry murals fill a whole courtyard, and in 1922 it became the first museum in the US to buy a painting by Van Gogh.

What to see:

  • Detroit Industry murals (Diego Rivera)
  • Self-Portrait (Vincent van Gogh)
  • The Wedding Dance (Pieter Bruegel the Elder)
  • The Nightmare (Henry Fuseli)

The museum's website

8. The Getty Center

Los Angeles · founded 1997 · Free (parking is charged)

A hilltop campus by Richard Meier with gardens and views across Los Angeles, and a fine collection of European paintings.

What to see:

  • Irises (Vincent van Gogh)
  • Wheatstacks, Snow Effect, Morning (Claude Monet)
  • Portrait of a Halberdier (Pontormo)
  • Arii Matamoe (Paul Gauguin)

The museum's website

9. The Barnes Foundation

Philadelphia · founded 1922

Albert C. Barnes hung his collection in dense wall arrangements, and it is still shown exactly his way: 181 Renoirs, 69 Cézannes and 59 Matisses.

What to see:

  • The Card Players (Paul Cézanne)
  • Models (Georges Seurat)
  • Le Bonheur de vivre (Henri Matisse)
  • The Dance mural (Henri Matisse)

The museum's website

10. The Frick Collection

New York · founded 1935

Henry Clay Frick's Fifth Avenue mansion, where Old Masters hang as they would in a private house.

What to see:

  • three paintings by Johannes Vermeer
  • Self-Portrait (Rembrandt, 1658)
  • Sir Thomas More (Hans Holbein the Younger)
  • Saint Francis in the Desert (Giovanni Bellini)

The museum's website

11. Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum

Boston · founded 1903

A Venetian-style palace built by a collector and kept as she arranged it. Empty frames still mark the 13 works stolen in 1990.

What to see:

  • El Jaleo (John Singer Sargent)
  • The Rape of Europa (Titian)
  • Portrait of Isabella Stewart Gardner (John Singer Sargent)

The museum's website

12. Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art

Kansas City · founded 1933 · Free

Free, with a renowned collection of Asian art and giant shuttlecocks on its lawn.

What to see:

  • Saint John the Baptist in the Wilderness (Caravaggio)
  • Water Lilies (Claude Monet)
  • Shuttlecocks (Claes Oldenburg and Coosje van Bruggen)

The museum's website

13. Kimbell Art Museum

Fort Worth · founded 1972 · Free to see the permanent collection

Small, superb and housed in Louis Kahn's celebrated vaulted building.

What to see:

  • The Torment of Saint Anthony (Michelangelo, his first known painting)
  • The Cardsharps (Caravaggio)

The museum's website

14. Smithsonian American Art Museum

Washington, DC · Free

The national collection of American art, free, in the historic Old Patent Office Building.

What to see:

  • Among the Sierra Nevada, California (Albert Bierstadt)
  • Elizabeth Winthrop Chanler (John Singer Sargent)
  • The Throne of the Third Heaven of the Nations' Millennium General Assembly (James Hampton)

The museum's website

Questions

What is the largest art museum in the US?

The Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York, the largest art museum in the Americas.

Which art museums in the US are free?

Among the great ones: the National Gallery of Art and the Smithsonian American Art Museum in Washington, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the Nelson-Atkins in Kansas City, the Getty Center in Los Angeles (you pay for parking) and the Kimbell in Fort Worth (for its permanent collection). The Detroit Institute of Arts is free for residents of its three home counties.

Where is the only Leonardo da Vinci painting in the Americas?

At the National Gallery of Art in Washington, DC: Ginevra de' Benci, a portrait of a young Florentine woman painted around 1474–78.
