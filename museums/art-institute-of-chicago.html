About the museum

The Art Institute was founded in 1879 and moved into its Michigan Avenue building, now guarded by two bronze lions, in 1893. Its Impressionist and Post-Impressionist collection is one of the finest outside Paris, built from Chicago collectors' gifts, and its American galleries hold two of the most famous paintings of the 20th century.

Nighthawks (1942) is still under copyright, so it isn't shown here, but it hangs in the American galleries near American Gothic. The European paintings below are mostly on the second floor.

Questions

What is the most famous painting at the Art Institute of Chicago?

Georges Seurat's A Sunday on La Grande Jatte (1884–86). Edward Hopper's Nighthawks and Grant Wood's American Gothic are the other two most famous.

Is Nighthawks at the Art Institute of Chicago?

Yes. Edward Hopper's Nighthawks (1942) has been in the museum's collection since the year it was painted.

Does the Art Institute of Chicago have Monet paintings?

Yes, one of the largest collections of Monet's work outside France, including several of his stacks of wheat and water lilies.
