The most famous paintings at the Cleveland Museum of Art include J. M. W. Turner's The Burning of the Houses of Lords and Commons, Frederic Edwin Church's Twilight in the Wilderness, Caravaggio's The Crucifixion of Saint Andrew, Pablo Picasso's La Vie and a panel of Claude Monet's Water Lilies.

About the museum

The Cleveland Museum of Art opened in 1916 in University Circle and has been free to everyone since. It also put its collection online under open access, which is why so many of its paintings can be shown here.

La Vie and the Caravaggio aren't in the app's catalog, so they aren't shown below, but both hang in the permanent galleries.

Questions

Is the Cleveland Museum of Art free?

Yes. General admission has been free since the museum opened in 1916. Some special exhibitions are ticketed.

What is the most famous painting at the Cleveland Museum of Art?

J. M. W. Turner's The Burning of the Houses of Lords and Commons (1835) and Frederic Edwin Church's Twilight in the Wilderness (1860) are among the best known, with Picasso's La Vie.

Can I download the Cleveland Museum of Art's paintings?

Yes. The museum releases its public-domain works under Creative Commons Zero, free for any use, and every painting on this page can be downloaded as a wallpaper.
