About the museum

The National Gallery was created by Congress in 1937 with Andrew Mellon's collection as its foundation, and opened on the National Mall in 1941. Admission is always free. The West Building holds European and American paintings through the 19th century; the East Building, by I. M. Pei, holds modern art.

Ginevra de' Benci isn't in the app's catalog, so it isn't shown below, but it hangs in the West Building's Italian galleries.

Questions

Is the National Gallery of Art free?

Yes. Admission to the National Gallery of Art is always free, and so is admission to the Smithsonian museums on the Mall.

Does the National Gallery of Art have a Leonardo da Vinci?

Yes, Ginevra de' Benci (about 1474–78), the only painting by Leonardo in the Americas.

What is the difference between the National Gallery of Art and the National Gallery in London?

They are different museums. The National Gallery of Art is in Washington, DC; the National Gallery is on Trafalgar Square in London.
